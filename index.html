From blank page to finished comment in four steps.

  1. Add your students

    Enter each student's name, pronouns and year level. The app handles the rest automatically.

  2. Set grades

    Assign A–E grades per strand for English and Maths. The sentence bank filters to show only grade-appropriate comments.

  3. Select sentences

    Click sentences from the bank to build your comment. Edit freely, or use AI Polish to improve flow in one click.

  4. Copy and paste

    Hit Copy in the Review view. Your polished, professional report comment is ready to paste into your school's reporting system.

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Every feature was designed around the real workflow of a classroom teacher at report time.

  • Approved sentence bank

    Every sentence has been reviewed and approved. Build comments by clicking, with no typing required. Sentences automatically adapt to each student's name and pronouns.

  • Name and pronoun substitution

    Set each student's name, pronouns and year level once. The app alternates between name and pronoun across sentences for natural, flowing comments.

  • AI-powered polish

    A one-click Polish button rewrites your comment for flow and cohesion, merging related sentences and varying openers, while keeping every piece of content intact.

  • Issue checker

    Automatically flags duplicate ideas, contradictions, tense inconsistencies and future directions that conflict with stated strengths, before you copy.

  • Grade-filtered sentences

    Set a grade for each strand and the sentence bank filters to show only appropriate comments. Select multiple grades to see sentences across a range.

  • Future directions

    Choose up to two future directions per subject from a stage-appropriate bank. Auto-suggested based on the student's lowest-graded strands.

  • Semester 1 and 2 support

    Switch between semesters for each student. Semester 1 comments are snapshotted for reference when building Semester 2, so no data is ever lost.

  • Cloud sync

    Your session saves automatically to the cloud every 15 seconds. Sign in on any device or browser and your students and comments are right there.

  • Fully editable

    Every comment can be edited freely in the draft pane. Synonym suggestions, manual rewrites and AI improvements all carry through to the final version.
